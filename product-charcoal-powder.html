Manufacturing & Metallurgy

Commercial Applications

Metallurgy & Recarburizing

High fixed carbon (>75%) and ultra-low sulfur make it an ideal carbon raiser and recarburizer in high-purity alloy steel smelting and foundries.

Cosmetics & Personal Care

Fine 200–300 mesh pulverized texture is used as an active adsorptive ingredient in detox face masks, peel-off scrubs, and natural soaps.

Briquette Manufacturing Feed

Consistent particle granulometry allows uniform mixing with organic starch binders for high-density combustible BBQ & shisha briquettes.

Soil Conditioning & Agriculture

High porosity biochar amendment that enhances soil aeration, prevents nutrient leaching, and accelerates beneficial microbial colonization.
