Coconut Expeller Cake (Copra Meal)

The premium solid co-product obtained from the mechanical expeller extraction of pure edible copra in Tiptur, Karnataka. Completely solvent-free and non-GMO, our copra meal provides an ideal combination of 18–22% plant-based protein, highly digestible fiber, and rich residual coconut fats that accelerate lactation and weight gain in livestock.

High Digestible Energy: 7–10% residual coconut oil provides safe, non-acidotic rumen bypass energy.
100% Chemical & Solvent Free: Pure physical expeller pressing without hexane, chemical binders, or additives.
Rigid Aflatoxin Control: Freshly crushed from sun-dried copra, tested to guarantee Aflatoxin B1 < 20 ppb.
