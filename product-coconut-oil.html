Pure Coconut Oil (Mechanical Expeller Grade)

Extracted by heavy mechanical screw expellers from mature, sun-dried Tiptur copra. Triple-filtered to achieve a radiant golden clarity and a deep, authentic roasted coconut aroma. Renowned for its high heat resistance, stability, and chemical neutrality, making it the choice medium for large-scale food processors, bakeries, and industrial soap makers globally.

No Chemical Solvents: 100% mechanical pressure extraction without hexane or synthetic solvent processing.
High Thermal Stability: High smoke point (~232°C / 450°F) ideal for continuous commercial deep frying and confectionery.
Low Acid Number: Carefully managed during expelling to preserve low FFA and prevent hydrolytic rancidity.
