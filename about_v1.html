01

The Visionary:
B Manjula

RBN EXIM was founded with a singular, unwavering vision: to bring the unadulterated purity of traditional Indian agriculture to the global stage. Understanding the immense potential and health benefits of authentic coconut products, the Kalpavriksha brand was established.

Under her leadership, RBN EXIM has grown from deep local roots into a powerhouse exporting premium goods to Singapore, Malaysia, the UAE, and beyond. Her commitment ensures every drop of oil and gram of copra meets rigorous international standards.

Strategic Origin

Why Tiptur?

We don't just source coconuts from anywhere. Our headquarters and manufacturing facilities are proudly located in Tiptur, Tumkur District, Karnataka.

Known globally as the World's No. 1 Copra Market, Tiptur's unique soil and climate yield coconuts with an exceptionally sweet taste and high oil content.

"By operating at the very heart of coconut agriculture, we cut out middlemen, ensuring farm-to-shipment freshness and quality control that others simply cannot match."
