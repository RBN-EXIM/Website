Granulated Coconut Shell Charcoal Chips

Cleanly carbonized pieces of dense mature coconut shells sourced from Tiptur, Karnataka. Screened to remove dirt and unburnt husks, these granular chips deliver high mechanical hardness, exceptionally high calorific value (>7,200 kcal/kg), and virtually zero smoke. The primary global raw material for hookah briquette manufacturers and steam-activated carbon production.

Optimal Pore Structure: Superior internal micro-porosity makes it ideal for direct steam-gas thermal activation.
Long Burn Duration: High density provides sustained thermal output with minimal ash generation (<3%).
De-Dusted & Screened: Mechanically passed through rotary trommel screens to ensure uniform granulometry.

Heavy Industry & Processing

Commercial Applications

Granular Activated Carbon

Prime raw material for rotary steam activation kilns, yielding high-iodine (>1,050 mg/g) granular carbon for water and air treatment.

Hookah & Shisha Briquettes

Screened chip size ensures uniform compaction, long burn time (90+ min), and odorless white ash for premium shisha cubes.

Precious Metal Metallurgy

Exceptional mechanical hardness (>98%) minimizes attrition losses in Carbon-in-Pulp (CIP) and Carbon-in-Leach (CIL) gold recovery circuits.

Industrial Gas Scrubbing

Superior micro-pore distribution provides immediate adsorption capacity for VOCs, solvent recovery, and gas filtration.
