The Kalpavriksha Heritage

Rooted in Purity.
Exporting to the World.

We bridge the gap between traditional Indian agricultural purity and modern global export standards.

B Manjula

Founder & Visionary

RBN EXIM was founded with a singular vision: to bring the unadulterated purity of traditional Indian agriculture to the global stage. Understanding the immense health benefits of coconut products, Kalpavriksha was born.

Under her leadership, we have grown from local roots in the Copra Capital into a global powerhouse, safely exporting premium goods to Singapore, Malaysia, the UAE, and beyond.

The Process

The Tiptur Advantage

1

Direct Sourcing

Operating at the heart of the World's No. 1 Copra Market. Hand-selected directly from local Tiptur farms for maximum sweetness and oil content, cutting out the middlemen entirely.

2

Cold-Pressed Excellence

State-of-the-art machinery extracts the oils slowly and naturally. This preserves the natural aroma, distinct flavor, and immense nutritional value in every drop.

3

Global Reach

Strict hygiene protocols and international packaging standards guarantee that our pristine, unadulterated quality safely reaches Singapore, UAE, and beyond.

Delivering India's finest agricultural products and premium coconut exports to the global market.
