Premium Tiptur Copra (Edible & Milling Grade)

Tiptur copra is internationally celebrated for its uniquely thick coconut meat, sweet aroma, and remarkably high oil retention (>68%). Sourced directly from our family farm aggregation networks in Tumkur District, our half-cups are naturally sun-dried without chemical sulfur burning, ensuring pristine organic purity for edible consumption and oil milling.

Zero Sulfur Smoke: Naturally dried under clean tropical sun; 100% free from carcinogenic sulfur residue.
Moisture Controlled (<6%): Rigorously cured to prevent transit mold, fungal sweat, and fatty acid degradation.
Hand-Graded Half Cups: Mechanically cleaned and sorted into uniform half-cup sizes without broken debris.
